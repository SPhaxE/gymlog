# Nano Banana 提示词 · 初见引导：米洛（Milo）的故事

> 2026-10-06 · 分镜见 `design/brand/story/storyboard.md`。人物和场景是新角色，但必须和现有 IP 小牛是**同一套画法**（平涂几何、无描边、同一套颜色），放在一起像同一部动画。

## 用法

1. **每次都上传参考图**：
   - 风格：`docs/sources/brand-refs/ip2-geo-b-selected.jpg`；
   - 牛的定妆：`docs/sources/mascot/A.jpg`；
   - 扛着的牛：`docs/sources/mascot/B1.jpg`（牛犊）、`docs/sources/mascot/B2.jpg`（小牛）、`docs/sources/mascot/B3.jpg`（壮牛）、`docs/sources/mascot/B4.jpg`（公牛）。
2. **按顺序出**：
   - **第一步 · 定妆板**（深色底，3 × 2）：确认米洛长什么样。挑满意的一张发我，我先看一眼再往下。
   - **第二步 · 单张**（品红底，6 张）：每张一个姿势，进 App 用。上传参考图 + 选中的定妆板。
   - **第三步 · 场景**（3 张分层 + 1 张拱门）。
3. 出好放进 `docs/sources/story/`，文件名用下面每段标题里的编号（如 `M2.png`、`S1-mid.png`），或直接发到对话里。

## 设定速查（第一步到第三步通用）

- **米洛**：古希腊摔跤手，力量训练的祖师爷。
  - 宽肩、厚胸、粗脖子、短卷胡、头顶一个小发髻，戴一圈**荧光桂冠**（全身唯一的荧光）。
  - 腰间围一块灰色短布（希腊式缠腰），赤足或简单凉鞋。
  - 性格：强壮、沉稳、有点憨，和小牛一样「可爱但不幼稚、强壮但不吓人」。
- **画法和小牛完全一样**：
  - 平涂几何，像现代体育图标，每个部位由圆、半圆、新月、胶囊拼成；
  - 无描边、无渐变、无纹理、无阴影；前后层次只靠深浅不同的平涂色叠出来。
- **脸**：身体侧面朝右，头转四分之三朝向观者，**只看得到一只眼**（和小牛一样的约定）。
- **颜色只用这几种**：

| 用途 | 颜色 |
|---|---|
| 皮肤近侧 | 骨白 `#E9E2D0` |
| 皮肤远侧（远侧手臂、腿） | 浅米 `#C9C2B2` |
| 胡子、头发、缠腰布 | 暖中灰 `#AAA59B` / `#928D87` |
| 眼睛 | 近黑 `#1E1D1A` |
| 桂冠，以及肩上牛的角 | 荧光 `#D4FF3A` |

- **肩上的牛**：就是参考图里的 IP 小牛，同样的画法、同样的单眼和荧光角，只是被扛着（四条腿垂在米洛胸前和背后）。
- **身高**：米洛站直约是成年公牛身高的 1.6 倍。

## 第一步 · 定妆板（深色底）

```text
Use the attached images as the exact style reference (first image) and the exact mascot reference (the other images: the bull mascot at different ages — newborn calf, young calf, sturdy young bull, full-grown bull).

Character expression sheet of ONE new character drawn in EXACTLY the same style as the bull mascot: MILO OF CROTON, the legendary ancient Greek wrestler and father of strength training, for the app "慢牛 Milo".

STYLE (identical to the reference):
- Flat solid geometric vector illustration, like a modernist sports pictogram. Every body part is built from a few simple shapes: circles, half-circles, crescents and capsules (two circles joined by straight tangent lines). Torso = a big rounded chest shape; arms and legs = tapered capsules; hands and feet = simple rounded shapes; head = a rounded shape with a short curly beard made of a few overlapping circles and a small hair bun on top.
- Pure flat fills, perfectly crisp smooth edges. NO outlines, NO strokes, NO gradients, NO texture, NO shading, NO drop shadows, NO ground shadow. Overlaps shown only by layering flat shapes of different tones (far-side arm and leg slightly darker).
- Palette, use ONLY these: bone white #E9E2D0 (near-side skin), light beige #C9C2B2 (far-side skin), warm mid grey #AAA59B (beard, hair), warm grey #928D87 (a short wrestler's loincloth wrapped at the waist), near-black #1E1D1A (eye), electric lime #D4FF3A (ONLY the laurel wreath on his head, and the horns of the bull mascot).
- Body in side profile facing RIGHT; the head turned three-quarters toward the viewer, ONLY ONE EYE visible (a simple near-black dot), friendly calm expression. Very broad shoulders, thick chest, thick neck, strong arms and legs — powerful but friendly, slightly goofy, never scary. Barefoot.
- The bull mascot, when present, is EXACTLY the referenced mascot at the stated age (same shapes, single eye, lime horns), carried across Milo's shoulders behind his neck, its four legs hanging down in front of and behind his shoulders, Milo's hands holding its legs.
- Milo standing upright is about 1.6 times the height of the full-grown bull.

LAYOUT: a 3 × 2 grid, every figure the same scale, standing on the same ground line within its row. No labels.
Top row, left to right:
1. M1 STANDING PROUD: Milo alone, standing tall, fists on his hips, chest out, chin up, laurel wreath.
2. M2 LIFTING THE CALF: Milo bending his knees and swinging the NEWBORN CALF (smallest stage) up onto his shoulders, the calf looking surprised.
3. M3 WALKING WITH THE YOUNG CALF: Milo walking to the right with an easy stride, the YOUNG CALF stage across his shoulders, both look content.
Bottom row, left to right:
4. M4 WALKING WITH THE STURDY BULL: Milo walking with effort — knees bent, body leaning forward, jaw set — the STURDY YOUNG BULL across his shoulders.
5. M5 CARRYING THE FULL-GROWN BULL: Milo standing tall and triumphant with the FULL-GROWN BULL across his shoulders, both heads up, proud.
6. M6 RESTING: Milo sitting on the ground, back against a simple flat stone column, asleep (one closed eye shown as a thin downward arc), the NEWBORN CALF lying folded beside him asleep too, two small warm-grey "z" letters above them (the only letters allowed).

Wide landscape 16:9, 4K resolution.
BACKGROUND: perfectly flat solid near-black #0A0A0B, no vignette, no gradient, no texture, no frame.
AVOID: realistic human anatomy, muscles drawn with lines, six-pack lines, skin tones other than the given palette, modern clothing, gym equipment, red cape, bullfighting, 3D render, clay, plush, glossy highlights, outlines, sketch lines, extra limbs, a second visible eye, any text other than the two z's, logos, watermark, frame, border.
```

## 第二步 · 单张（品红底，进 App 用）

每张单独出，上传风格参考、牛的参考、**第一步选中的定妆板**。下面是公共前缀，后面接每张的那一句。

公共前缀：

```text
Use the attached images as the exact style and character reference (the character sheet is the approved design of Milo — keep him identical: same proportions, beard, hair bun, laurel wreath, colors; keep the bull mascot identical to its reference).
ONE single figure (Milo, with the bull mascot if stated), centered, occupying about 70% of the frame width, feet standing on an invisible ground line at about 85% of the image height. Same flat geometric style and palette as the references: no outlines, no gradients, no shading, no shadows, no glow, no ground shadow.
Square 1:1, 2048 × 2048.
BACKGROUND: one perfectly flat, uniform solid pure magenta #FF00FF, edge to edge, no gradient, no vignette, no texture, no shadow. No magenta, pink or purple anywhere on the character.
AVOID: any text or letters, logos, watermark, frame, extra characters, props not described.
```

每张接一句：

- **M1**：`Pose: Milo standing tall alone, fists on his hips, chest out, chin up, calm proud smile.`
- **M2**：`Pose: Milo bending his knees and swinging the NEWBORN CALF up onto his shoulders; the calf looks surprised.`
- **M3**：`Pose: Milo walking to the right with an easy stride, the YOUNG CALF across his shoulders, both content.`
- **M4**：`Pose: Milo walking to the right with visible effort — knees bent, leaning forward, jaw set — the STURDY YOUNG BULL across his shoulders.`
- **M5**：`Pose: Milo standing tall and triumphant with the FULL-GROWN BULL across his shoulders, both heads raised proudly.`
- **M6**：`Pose: Milo sitting on the ground asleep, back leaning against a simple flat stone column (warm grey #928D87), one closed eye as a thin downward arc; the NEWBORN CALF lying folded asleep beside him. No z letters (the app animates them).`

## 第三步 · 场景

做视差用，三层分开出。中景、近景要透明，所以用品红底；远景铺满，用深色。全部只用暗色（会放在近黑的 App 背景上），不要亮色，**不要荧光**（荧光留给人物和 UI）。

```text
Use the attached style reference: the same flat geometric pictogram style (no outlines, no gradients, no texture, no shading, crisp shapes).
Scene layer for a parallax intro animation set in ancient Greece (Croton by the sea, 6th century BC). DARK, LOW-CONTRAST palette only: near-black #0A0A0B, very dark warm greys #141312, #1C1B19, #24221F, #2E2C28. No bright colors, no lime, no white, no people, no animals, no text.
Wide panoramic 3:1, highest resolution.
```

每层接一句：

- **S1-far**（远景，铺满，不抠图）：`Layer: FAR background, full-bleed: a calm sea horizon with gentle rounded hills and a large soft circular moon shape, all in the darkest greys. Background fills the whole image (no magenta).`
- **S1-mid**（中景，品红底）：`Layer: MIDDLE: a row of simple Doric temple columns with a flat roof beam on the left third, two rounded olive trees on the right, low stone wall between them, all flat dark greys. BACKGROUND: perfectly flat pure magenta #FF00FF behind everything (to be keyed out).`
- **S1-near**（近景，品红底）：`Layer: NEAR foreground: a flat ground strip along the bottom 15% of the image with a few rounded stones and tufts of grass shapes at the edges, dark greys. BACKGROUND: perfectly flat pure magenta #FF00FF above the ground (to be keyed out).`
- **S2**（奥林匹亚拱门，品红底，1:1）：`Square 1:1 instead of 3:1. Layer: the stone arched tunnel entrance of the ancient Olympia stadium, seen from the front, simple flat stone blocks, the inside of the arch slightly lighter (#2E2C28) as if light comes through. BACKGROUND: perfectly flat pure magenta #FF00FF around it (to be keyed out).`

## 检查清单

- 米洛和小牛同一套画法：无描边、无渐变，只有一只眼，只有桂冠和牛角是荧光。
- 肩上的牛就是 IP 小牛，牛龄对得上（牛犊 / 小牛 / 壮牛 / 公牛）。
- 单张的品红底均匀，人物身上没有粉紫色；脚踩在同一高度。
- 场景只用暗色，没有人物、动物和文字。
